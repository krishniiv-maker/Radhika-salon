OUR STORY

A Dream That
Became A Legacy.

What began as a childhood dream grew into a salon built with passion, dedication and the belief of creating something of her own.

With a natural interest in beauty since childhood, Radhika ji always dreamed of building something of her own. After working in a semi-government office, she decided to take that dream forward — saving, learning the craft and preparing herself to take the first step.

In April 2011, that dream became a reality when Radhika's Salon opened its doors. What followed was more than just the growth of a salon. It was the beginning of relationships that have lasted for years.

Many of the clients who walked in during the early days are still a part of the salon today. The team, too, has always been treated like family — creating a space built not only around beauty, but around trust and genuine care.
